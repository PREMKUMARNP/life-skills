## Question 2: Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines

A growth mindset means believing that we can improve our skills and abilities through learning, practice, and effort. Instead of thinking that we cannot do something, we should be willing to learn and try again.

## Question 3: What is Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions, decisions, and efforts have an influence on our lives. Instead of blaming others or situations when something goes wrong, we should look at what we can change and take responsibility for it. The main point is that our actions can make a difference in the outcome.
