## Question 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?
### Unwanted Comments and Behaviour:
Sexual harassment includes making unwanted comments, sexual jokes, remarks about someone's appearance, or using words that make another person feel uncomfortable. It can also include unwanted touching, inappropriate gestures, or invading someone's personal space without consent.
###  Online and Repeated Harassment:
Sending unwanted messages, sharing offensive pictures, repeatedly calling someone, or continuing to approach someone despite their discomfort can also be considered harassment. Such behaviour can happen in the workplace or through online communication.
###  Misuse of Authority and Disrespect:
Using one's position to pressure someone, asking for personal favours in return for professional benefits, or threatening someone for rejecting such requests is unacceptable. Such behaviour can affect a person's confidence, mental health, and work environment. Everyone should respect others' boundaries and maintain professionalism.
## Question 2. What would you do if you faced or witnessed any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?
### Speak Up and Support the Person:
If I faced or witnessed such behaviour, I would take it seriously and not ignore it. If possible, I would tell the person to stop and make it clear that their behaviour is unacceptable. If someone else was affected, I would support them, listen to them, and encourage them to speak up without blaming or judging them.
###  Keep Evidence and Report the Incident:
I would keep a record of what happened, including the date, time, and place. If there were any messages, emails, or screenshots, I would save them as evidence. If the incident was serious or repeated, I would report it to HR or the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) so that appropriate action could be taken.
###  Maintain Confidentiality and Workplace Safety:
I would avoid spreading the incident among others and respect the privacy of everyone involved. I would cooperate if an investigation was conducted and ensure that the matter was handled responsibly. I believe everyone deserves a safe and respectful workplace, and we should take responsibility for preventing such behaviour.
