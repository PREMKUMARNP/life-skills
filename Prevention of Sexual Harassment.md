## Question 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes making unwanted comments, jokes, gestures, or remarks about someone's appearance. It can also involve unwanted touching, sending offensive messages or pictures, making sexual remarks, or repeatedly disturbing someone even after they show discomfort. Such behaviour can happen in person, over phone calls, or through social media. Sometimes people may think they are joking or having fun, but their actions can make others feel uncomfortable, embarrassed, or unsafe. Making someone feel pressured, ignoring their boundaries, or treating them disrespectfully is unacceptable. Everyone should understand the importance of personal space, mutual respect, and professional behaviour. A workplace should be a place where people can work freely without fear, discomfort, or discrimination.

## Question 2. What would you do if you faced or witnessed any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

If I faced or witnessed such behaviour, I would take it seriously and would not ignore it. I would try to handle the situation calmly and, if possible, tell the person that their behaviour is not acceptable. If someone else was affected, I would support them, listen to them, and encourage them to speak up instead of staying silent. If the incident was serious or happened repeatedly, I would report it to HR or the Internal Complaints Committee . I would also keep any messages, emails, or other evidence related to the incident. I would avoid spreading the matter unnecessarily and respect the privacy of the people involved. I believe everyone has the right to work in a safe environment where they feel respected, comfortable, and treated equally. Maintaining a healthy and respectful workplace is everyone's responsibility.
