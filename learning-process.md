### Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a way of learning by explaining a concept in our own simple words to make sure we truly understand it.

### Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

**Focused Mode:** This happens when we give our full attention to one task and work on it without distractions. For example, writing a Java program or debugging code.

**Diffuse Mode:** This happens when we are relaxed and allow our thoughts to move freely. It helps us find new ideas and understand difficult problems. For example, thinking about a coding problem while walking or listening to music.

### Question 4: According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

1. First, identify the skill or subject you want to learn.
2. Understand the basic concepts before moving to advanced topics.
3. Divide the learning process into manageable tasks.
4. Spend time practicing and identify where you are making mistakes.
5. Stay consistent and dedicate sufficient time to improve your skills.
