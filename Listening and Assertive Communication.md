Question 1: What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

1. Pay Attention: Focus on the speaker.
2. Avoid Distractions: Stay away from mobile phones.
3. Don't Interrupt: Let others finish speaking.
4. Maintain Eye Contact: Look at the speaker.
5. Ask Questions: Clear your doubts.
6. Show Interest: Nod your head while listening.
7. Take Notes: Write important points.
8. Give Feedback: Repeat the main points.

Question 2: According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

1. Listen Carefully: Give full attention to the speaker.
2. Understand Feelings: Identify the speaker's emotions.
3. Observe Body Language: Notice facial expressions.
4. Remember Important Points: Focus on the main information.
5. Repeat the Message: Explain what you understood.
6. Take Notes: Write down important details.
7. Verify Information: Confirm that you understood correctly.

Question 5: When do you switch to Passive Communication Style in your day to day life?

1. Feeling Shy: When I feel shy to express my opinions.
2. Fear of Mistakes: When I am afraid of making mistakes.
3. Avoiding Arguments: When I don't want to argue.
4. Accepting Decisions: When I agree with others' decisions even though I have a different opinion.
5. Saying No: When I find it difficult to refuse others.

Question 6: When do you switch to Aggressive Communication Style in your day to day life?

1. Anger: When someone makes me angry.
2. Frustration: When things don't happen as expected.
3. Interruptions: When someone repeatedly disturbs me.
4. Arguments: When someone disagrees with me.
5. Lack of Patience: When I lose control of my emotions.

Question 7: When do you switch to Passive Aggressive Communication Style in your day to day life?

1. Feeling Hurt: When someone hurts my feelings.
2. Silence: When I avoid talking because I am angry.
3. Sarcasm: When I use indirect comments to express anger.
4. Ignoring Others: When I ignore someone because I am upset.
5. Indirect Replies: When I give short replies instead of explaining my feelings.

Question 8: How can you make your communication assertive?

1. Be Confident: I will express my thoughts without fear.
2. Speak Clearly: I will use simple and respectful words.
3. Respect Others: I will respect everyone's opinions.
4. Control Emotions: I will stay calm during disagreements.
5. Say No Politely: I will refuse when I am uncomfortable.
6. Maintain Eye Contact: I will show confidence through body language.
7. Express Feelings: I will share my feelings honestly.
8. Avoid Blaming: I will explain problems without blaming others.
